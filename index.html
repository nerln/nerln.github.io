Eugenio Giuseppe Nerelli

I build developer tools and desktop apps, and I publish the source. There are 28 public repositories on github.com/nerln, written in Python, Swift, JavaScript, HTML and CSS. All of it runs on a laptop and keeps its data there, with no telemetry and no accounts.

Nearly half of them belong to one suite: tools for running several coding agents, Claude Code and Codex, on one Mac without them colliding. A task board, a shared blackboard, a queue for heavy jobs with an AI judge, files an agent cannot read. Next to them are native apps in Swift and Python, for macOS, iOS, Windows and Android.

I am also a PhD student at the University of València, moving into mechanistic interpretability, after earlier work on immersive VR. Two papers from that work are published.
