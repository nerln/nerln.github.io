  • contigo

    Learn a language, with you. Alone first, then with someone you love.

    Web

    What it does
    • Each unit starts with a task you do alone, and every answer is corrected at once. The first course is English, for people who speak Spanish.
    • You listen to each phrase, also slower, and you record yourself. The recording never leaves your phone.
    • The person teaching opens the class and sees, with your permission, what you did and what you missed.

    Where it runs. A web app in the browser. The iPhone app is not in the App Store yet.

  • FERRO

    A web app for workouts: plans, sets and the gym turnstile badge.

    Web

    What it does
    • 233 exercises with a video, described in Italian, English and Spanish.
    • The gym turnstile code is generated on the phone, so the badge works with no signal.
    • You can send a whole workout to someone as a link or a QR code. It needs no server and no account.

    Where it runs. A web app for iPhone, added to the home screen.

  • vesta

    Try your clothes on a photo of yourself. Made with Gianmattia Barone.

    Web · macOS

    What it does
    • It runs in the browser, with no account. No photo leaves the device.
    • A Mac version produces a try-on image in about ninety seconds.
    • Colour analysis of the skin in CIELAB suggests a season and a palette.

    Where it runs. A browser app, also installable on the iPhone home screen. The Mac version needs Apple Silicon.

    SiteCodeDocs

  • dins el quadre

    The museum souvenir photo, built from two phone shots.

    macOS

    What it does
    • The scale is calculated from the artwork's real height on the label, not guessed.
    • It finds the painting's four corners with a Hough line transform, so a slanted shot still works.
    • It cuts the visitor out and moves their colours towards the light of the room.

    Where it runs. Runs locally: a Python backend and a web page that needs only a browser and a camera. It has a site for museums. A few points are still open before it goes into a room.
