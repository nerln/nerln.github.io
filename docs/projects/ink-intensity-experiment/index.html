ink-intensity-experiment

Two published derivations of one CT scan of PHerc 0172 give different ink maps when the same model is run on both. This repository tests one cause for that difference: the 8-bit intensity scale each derivation was written with. It is the causal experiment for ScrollPrize/villa#1372, and it is written for anyone running or reading ink detection on the published volumes, where the choice of derivation is rarely deliberate.

The scale accounts for almost all of the disagreement. Δ@5%, the disagreement between the top 5% of each map, falls from 0.757 to 0.045 when one derivation’s intensity is remapped onto the other’s using coefficients measured off the evaluated region, a 94.0% reduction. Rank correlation goes from 0.510 to 0.999.

Δ@1% Δ@5% Δ@20% ρ
control: A vs A 0.000 0.000 0.000 1.000
arm 1: A vs B 0.865 0.757 0.650 0.510
arm 2: A vs remap(B) 0.037 0.044 0.031 0.999
reference: B vs remap(B) 0.869 0.757 0.648 0.512

A is PHerc0172/volumes/20241024131838-…, B is …131839-…. Under the other blending convention the reduction is 96.6%, from 0.771 to 0.026.

What it does not establish

Section titled “What it does not establish”

The cause is measured on one segment. Per the census in inkfloor, that segment is the entire population of same-model, different-derivation pairs in the published corpus on 8 August 2026, so the result is not extended beyond it.

It says nothing about which derivation reads better. Δ compares two outputs without scoring either, and scrolls have no infrared ground truth. The measurement is that the pipeline moves with an input nobody controls, and that a remap fitted independently removes most of that movement.

A first run used the scrollprize/PHerc.1667-iteration-0 checkpoint. It gave the same qualitative answer, a 96.8% reduction, and scored 0.081 on the validity gate against a pass mark of 0.285. It stays in the repository and in out/, outside the headline result.

Checking the numbers

Section titled “Checking the numbers”

The three ink maps are committed, so every figure above recomputes with numpy alone: no torch, no checkpoint, no renders, no network. About five seconds.

Running it

Section titled “Running it”

Everything runs on a laptop. Inference uses Apple MPS where available, and there is no CUDA requirement, no credentials, no bulk download.

Renders come from vc_render_tifxyz in ScrollPrize/villa. docs/rendering.md holds the command lines, the source of each parameter in the published .zattrs, and how the ROI was chosen. Weights, renders and the S3 cache are gitignored at 1.7 GB, 136 MB and 27 MB, all regenerable.

How it works

Section titled “How it works”

calibrate.py is a validity gate. The experiment prints no causal arms until it has reproduced a published prediction with the checkpoint that produced it, and the script exits non-zero on failure.

The remap used in arm 2, A = 0.6165*B + 104.02, is fitted voxel by voxel on the evaluated renders, which would make arm 2 circular on its own. fit_affine.py and sweep_affine.py refit it elsewhere.

coefficients Δ@5%
identity, no remap 0.757 arm 1 restated
intercept only 0.882 worse than doing nothing
slope only 0.594
fitted on these renders 0.044 in-sample
fitted on five volume chunks that never touch the ROI 0.045 out-of-sample

The in-sample fit is worth a tenth of a point out of ninety-four. Each half of the affine alone leaves most of the disagreement standing, and the offset alone makes it worse. The independent coefficients carry 99.31% of the region’s voxels to within one 8-bit level.

The depth window comes from the reference implementation. ink-detection/inference_timesformer.py defaults to start_idx=17 with CFG.in_chans=26, so the reference stack is legacy layers 17 through 42. A 33-slice render centred on the surface spans legacy 16 to 48, which puts 17 to 42 at local indices [1:27). Scored against the published map, [3:29) gives 0.395, [1:31) gives 0.420, and [1:27) gives 0.449.

What is verified

Section titled “What is verified”
IoU@5%
reproduction vs published july map 0.449
published july vs published november 0.284
chance 0.026

The reproduction resembles the july map more closely than the two published maps resemble each other, which ties the agreement to the checkpoint that was run.

The render matches the published surface volume on 99.75% of voxels, with a maximum difference of one unit in 255 and none of two. coverage/ enumerates chunk presence with ListObjectsV2, 96 seconds for the whole scroll and no payload downloaded.
